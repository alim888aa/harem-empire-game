IMPERIAL THRONE + FIVE-PANEL CARVED SCREEN
Harem Empire • authored in Blender 4.3.2 • 1 October 2026

WHAT THIS IS
Original, volumetric, Forbidden City-inspired decorative game art. This is an
interpretive model, not a measured museum replica or an AAA-quality claim.
The throne has rounded wooden scroll arms, gold edge trim, a shaped high back,
open side lattice, swept feet, and a jade-teal cushion. The stepped screen has
raised dragon, cloud and lotus motifs and geometric key-fret ornament. Motifs
are actual mesh relief rather than a rendered image or billboard.

COORDINATES AND INTEGRATION
GLB units are meters; +Y is up; +Z is forward. The floor pivot is directly under
the chair centre. The screen is behind the chair, centred at GLB Z=-1.40 m.
The chair cushion top is Y=0.67 m; its back is about 2.56 m high. Its footprint
is approximately 2.7 m wide by 1.8 m deep. Screen width is about 7.52 m and its
highest carving is about 5.93 m above the floor. Use asset_stats.json for exact
whole-asset bounds. Do not centre the imported bounding box: keep the origin.

Suggested placement for the current palace: (0, 0.56, -25), scale (1,1,1).
All eight runtime mesh origins and transforms are normalized to the floor pivot.
No dais, environment floor, camera or light is exported. The source .blend
contains a separate STUDIO collection for reviewing the model; this is excluded
from GLB export. No collision hull is supplied; use the game's simple chair /
screen colliders if required. In Three.js enable castShadow / receiveShadow on
meshes and retain their exported PBR materials. Preview lighting is separate.

PERFORMANCE
Target: <50,000 triangles, <8 MB GLB, embedded textures no larger than 1024.
Final measured figures are in asset_stats.json and glb_validation.json.
The GLB has eight material batches. The editable source deliberately retains
separate components and more authoring triangles. The PNG renders show the
merged and decimated runtime geometry, rendered with Cycles CPU, not imagegen.
Small relief is intended to read mainly in the chair and medium-distance screen
views. It is stylized ornament, not photogrammetry.

REBUILD
Requires Blender 4.3.2. No add-ons, paid services or external software are used.
Unzip the source archive into any folder and run:
blender -b --python build_imperial_throne.py
blender -b --python optimize_and_render.py
blender -b --python validate_glb.py
All paths in the scripts are relative to their own folder. The .blend contains
packed image maps, so it can also be opened and edited independently.
